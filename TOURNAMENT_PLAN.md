# Crosshair-Bench 7-Company Tournament Plan

This document specifies the 7-Company, 28-Entrant LLM + 4-Engine Tournament architecture, balanced opening book, batch manifest structure, and crash-proof persistence guarantees for **Crosshair-Bench**.

### Engine Actors (`ai-crosshair`)
- **Calibration Anchor (`1200.0` Elo):** `engine:az-puct-1` (raw 114K policy network prior $P(s,a)$, zero tree search)
- **Tournament Engine 1:** `engine:random-uct-50` (50-simulation uniform-rollout MCTS without neural guidance)
- **Tournament Engine 2:** `engine:az-puct-400` (400-simulation AlphaZero PUCT search; also serves as the per-turn shadow oracle)
- **Tournament Engine 3:** `engine:az-puct-4000` (4000-simulation AlphaZero PUCT ceiling anchor)

## 2. Opening Fairness & Color Balancing

In every 4-game pairing between Player A and Player B:
- Two balanced 2-ply openings (`"d4 f4"` and `"c4 f4"`) are used.
- Each opening is played twice with identical engine/oracle seeds:
  - Game 1: `d4 f4` with Player A as Red, Player B as Blue
  - Game 2: `d4 f4` with Player B as Red, Player A as Blue
  - Game 3: `c4 f4` with Player A as Red, Player B as Blue
  - Game 4: `c4 f4` with Player B as Red, Player A as Blue
- First-player advantage cancels out 100% across every 4-game mini-match.

For **Batch 0** (Engine Anchor Calibration), 8 balanced 2-ply openings (`"d4 f4"`, `"c4 f4"`, `"d4 d2"`, `"d4 b4"`, `"e4 c4"`, `"c4 e4"`, `"d3 d5"`, `"d5 d3"`) are played with Red/Blue swaps for **16 games per pair** (96 games total).

## 3. Five-Batch Tournament Structure (`manifests/`)

All manifests are deterministically generated by `python3 -m crosshair_bench.build_manifests`:

1. **Batch 0 (`manifests/batch_00_engine_anchors.json`) [96 games, $0.00 CPU]:**
   - All $\binom{4}{2} = 6$ pairs among `engine:az-puct-1`, `engine:random-uct-50`, `engine:az-puct-400`, and `engine:az-puct-4000` x 16 games per pair.
   - Completed calibration results:
     - `engine:az-puct-4000`: **2196.2 Elo** (`47-1` in Batch 0: `16-0` vs `az-puct-1`, `16-0` vs `random-uct-50`, `15-1` vs `az-puct-400`)
     - `engine:az-puct-400`: **1753.7 Elo** (`33-15` in Batch 0: `16-0` vs `az-puct-1`, `16-0` vs `random-uct-50`, `1-15` vs `az-puct-4000`)
     - `engine:az-puct-1`: **1200.0 Elo** (Fixed Anchor; `9-39` in Batch 0: `9-7` vs `random-uct-50`)
     - `engine:random-uct-50`: **1133.8 Elo** (`7-41` in Batch 0: `7-9` vs `az-puct-1`)
2. **Batch 1 (`manifests/batch_01_vs_engines.json`) [336 games]:**
   - All 28 LLM entrants vs the 3 Tournament Engines (`random-uct-50`, `az-puct-400`, `az-puct-4000`) x 4 games per pair.
   - Also split by opponent engine into `batch_01a_vs_uct50.json` (112 games), `batch_01b_vs_az400.json` (112 games), and `batch_01c_vs_az4000.json` (112 games) with identical `match_id`s.
3. **Batch 2 (`manifests/batch_02_thinking_delta.json`) [84 games]:**
   - **Part A (56 games):** All 14 intra-model `@high` vs `@low`/`@none` pairs x 4 games, directly measuring the Elo and move-quality lift of extended thinking within each model.
   - **Part B (28 games):** All 7 intra-company `Flagship@low`/`@none` vs `Flash@high` pairs x 4 games, testing whether test-time thinking allows a smaller model to beat its lab's flagship at low thinking.
4. **Batch 3 (`manifests/batch_03_flagship_rr.json`) [84 games]:**
   - Full 7-company round-robin ($\binom{7}{2} = 21$ pairs x 4 games) among all 7 Flagship models at `@high`.
5. **Batch 4 (`manifests/batch_04_flash_rr.json`) [84 games]:**
   - Full 7-company round-robin ($\binom{7}{2} = 21$ pairs x 4 games) among all 7 Flash models at `@high`.

## 4. Crash-Proof Persistence Guarantees

- **Per-Ply Checkpoints (`results/checkpoints/<match_id>.partial.json`):** Written atomically via `.tmp` + `os.replace` after every single turn. Resumed automatically from `len(match_doc["moves"])` if interrupted, then deleted once the final `results/matches/<match_id>.json` is written.
- **Append-Only Turn Ledger (`results/turns_log.jsonl`):** Every completed LLM turn is immediately appended with its timestamp, move, `az-puct-400` evaluation, and token/cost telemetry.
- **Live Batch Status (`results/batches/<batch_id>_status.json`):** Updated atomically as each match finishes, tracking completed count, failures, token usage, and cumulative USD spend.
